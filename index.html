MealTrain

When a friend has a baby or a surgery, everyone asks "what can I bring?" - and then three people bring lasagna on the same Tuesday. MealTrain is the tiny coordination layer: who brings which meal on which night.

Open MealTrain
  • One night, one meal - nights can only be claimed once.
  • Coverage at a glance - which nights are still open, and the next gap.
  • Variety check - the same dish twice gets flagged before it arrives.
  • Private by design - everything stays in your browser.
